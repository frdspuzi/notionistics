FAQ

Do I need to pay for Notion?

Not necessarily. Many small teams run fine on the free plan. If you need more members or features, I'll tell you upfront which plan fits instead of upselling you.

My team isn't techy. Will they actually use it?

That's the whole point. The setup is built around how your team already works, kept as simple as possible, and comes with training. If it's too complicated to use, I built it wrong.

Who owns the workspace?

You do. Everything is built in your own Notion account. When we're done, it's 100% yours.

Can you work with my existing Excel / Google Sheets data?

Yes. Migrating your existing data in is part of the job, so you don't start from zero.

Do you work with businesses outside Malaysia?

Yes, everything can be done remotely over video call.
